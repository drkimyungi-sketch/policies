Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 7, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how this personal automation application ("the App") handles information when it interacts with the TikTok API. This App is a private n8n workflow used solely to cross-post content from YouTube to TikTok.

2. Information We Access

To post content to TikTok, the App may access the following information through the TikTok API:

  • TikTok account credentials (via OAuth access tokens)
  • Basic account profile information required for posting
  • Video content sourced from YouTube for cross-posting purposes

3. How We Use Information

Information accessed through the TikTok API is used exclusively to upload and publish video content to the authorized TikTok account. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising purposes.

4. Data Storage

This application does not maintain a database or permanently store any personal data. OAuth tokens used to authenticate with TikTok are stored locally within the n8n automation environment and are not transmitted to any external server outside of the TikTok API.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any personal data with third parties. The only data transmission that occurs is between the App and the TikTok API for the purpose of content publishing.

6. Third-Party Services

This App connects to the following third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies:

  • TikTok Privacy Policy
  • Google / YouTube Privacy Policy

7. Revoking Access

You may revoke the App's access to your TikTok account at any time through your TikTok account settings under "Manage App Permissions."

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated at any time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

For any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the application operator directly.
