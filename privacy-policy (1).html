Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 7, 2026

1. Introduction

PrayNest ("we", "our", or "the app") is a Bible and prayer application. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you use PrayNest and its associated automation tools, including TikTok API integrations.

2. Information We Access

To operate the content automation workflow, PrayNest may access:

  • TikTok account credentials via OAuth access tokens
  • Basic TikTok account profile information required for posting
  • Video content sourced from YouTube for cross-posting purposes

3. How We Use Information

Information accessed through the TikTok API is used exclusively to upload and publish video content to the authorized TikTok account. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising purposes.

4. Data Storage

PrayNest does not maintain a database or permanently store personal data. OAuth tokens used to authenticate with TikTok are stored locally within the automation environment and are not transmitted to any external server outside of the TikTok API.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any personal data with third parties. The only data transmission that occurs is between the app and the TikTok API for the purpose of content publishing.

6. Third-Party Services

PrayNest connects to the following third-party services, governed by their own privacy policies:

  • TikTok Privacy Policy
  • Google / YouTube Privacy Policy

7. Revoking Access

You may revoke PrayNest's access to your TikTok account at any time through your TikTok account settings under "Manage App Permissions."

8. Children's Privacy

PrayNest does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can remove it.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated at any time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
